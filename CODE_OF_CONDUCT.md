
# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participation in the PocketMC community is expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Being respectful when discussing bugs, ideas, and technical disagreements.
- Providing clear, relevant, and constructive feedback.
- Respecting the time and decisions of the project maintainer and other contributors.
- Accepting that not every suggestion or contribution will be implemented.
- Giving appropriate credit to the original authors of code and other materials.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or repeatedly posting irrelevant content.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code or intentionally misleading technical reports.
- Claiming ownership of another person's work.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

PocketMC is an independent hobby project maintained by CesarDev / ByCesarDev.

The maintainer may edit, close, lock, or decline issues and pull requests that violate these standards, disrupt the project, or fall outside its scope.

Participation does not guarantee a response, review, acceptance, or continued access to project discussions.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be submitted privately to the project maintainer through an appropriate contact channel.

The maintainer will determine the appropriate response based on the circumstances. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, closure of discussions, or restriction of participation in project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other interactions directly associated with PocketMC.

Thank you for helping maintain a respectful and constructive project community.
